🌍 Project Overview

This project analyzes solar, atmospheric, and environmental data for Benin, Sierra Leone, and Togo to evaluate solar energy potential and operational risks for solar power deployment.

🔍 Key Analysis Focus

Solar irradiance trends (GHI, DNI, DHI)

Temperature impact on solar efficiency

Wind speed & gusts for structural safety

Humidity & soiling potential

Country comparison & investment feasibility
